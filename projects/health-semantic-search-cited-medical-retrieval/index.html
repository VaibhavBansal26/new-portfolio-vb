Health Semantic Search — Cited Medical Retrieval

Health AI

A self-hostable search system over public health documents with access-aware retrieval, hybrid ranking, source citations and refusal when evidence is insufficient.

Repository overview

Permission-aware, cited semantic search over public health documents. Built stage by stage, with every design decision and measurement logged. The goal is a product a health system could adopt: self-hostable, answers always cite their source, and "no supported answer" beats a guess. Live app: https://health-semantic-search.onrender.com (free tier: it sleeps when idle, so the first load can take a minute; search only, no cited answers, see below) Research write-up: docs/PAPER.md, a workshop-style draft with every number traced to ENGINEERING LOG.md Full app with cited answers: https://huggingface.co/spaces/Vaibhavbansal26/health-semantic-search-app (same React app, reranker and cited answers; free CPU, so answers take 3-6 s) Original Gradio demo: the Space Vaibhavbansal26/health-semantic-search (reranker, no accounts)
